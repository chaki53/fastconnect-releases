## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Which APK should I pick?</b></summary>

In 95% of cases — `arm64-v8a`. If your phone is older than 2018 or you are unsure — take `universal`, it works on everything (but is larger).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Android asks to "allow installation from unknown sources"</b></summary>

This is normal — we are not published on Google Play yet. Allow the installation in settings, install the APK, then you can turn it back off.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it safe?</b></summary>

Yes. The APK is signed with the same key, updates come only from this repository. Sensitive data (CVV, full card numbers, passwords) is **never** stored on the device or the server.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can't connect — what should I do?</b></summary>

1. Press "Refresh servers" in your account
2. Try switching the country in the server list
3. Check whether your device limit is exhausted (Account → Devices)
4. Contact support right from the app

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where is my referral link?</b></summary>

Account → "Invite a friend". There are two links: one for the Telegram bot and one for direct app installation.
</details>
